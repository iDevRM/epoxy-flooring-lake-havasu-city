What Is Epoxy Flooring in Lake Havasu City?

Epoxy flooring is a resin coating bonded to bare concrete. In a Lake Havasu City garage that usually means three layers: a base coat, a broadcast of colored vinyl flakes, and a clear topcoat that seals it all in. The result is a floor that wipes clean, doesn’t soak up oil, and hides the dust that blows in every time the door goes up.

Most of our calls come from homeowners with a two- or three-car garage, an RV or boat bay, or a golf cart that lives inside. Shops and service bays along Highway 95 call too. The question we hear most is “Why did my last coating peel?” Usually, nobody ground the concrete first.

When Do You Need a Garage Floor Coating?

  • Your old coating is peeling. Hardware-store kits rolled onto unground concrete tend to lift where tires sit, especially after the first summer.
  • The slab is stained or dusting. Bare concrete sheds a fine powder and drinks up oil, transmission fluid and pool chemicals.
  • You just bought or built a house. New construction in the Foothills or Desert Hills is the easiest time to coat, before the garage fills up.
  • The boat or RV bay looks rough. Trailer jacks, wet hulls and the sand that comes home from the lake are hard on a bare floor.
  • You’re getting ready to sell. A clean coated garage photographs well and is one less thing for a buyer to fix.

How Epoxy Floor Installation Works in Lake Havasu City

  1. Free on-site estimate. We measure, check for cracks and old coatings, and tape down a plastic sheet if we suspect moisture coming up through the slab.
  2. Diamond grinding. A walk-behind grinder with a vacuum opens up the concrete so the coating can grip it. This is the step DIY kits skip.
  3. Crack and joint repair. Cracks and spalls are ground out and filled so they don’t telegraph through.
  4. Base coat and flakes. The base goes down and flakes are broadcast to refusal, then scraped and vacuumed.
  5. Clear topcoat. A UV-stable polyaspartic topcoat seals it. Most two-car garages are done in one or two days.

Summer jobs start at first light, while the slab is still cool. Hot concrete makes the material set fast, and rushing it is how you end up with roller marks.

How Much Does Epoxy Flooring Cost in Lake Havasu City?

The price of a garage floor coating comes down to a handful of things we can see in a 20-minute visit:

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does garage floor epoxy last in Lake Havasu City?

A properly ground and coated garage floor usually lasts 10 to 20 years before it needs a fresh topcoat. The part that wears first in Lake Havasu City is the strip by the door that sits in direct sun. We finish most garages with a UV-stable polyaspartic topcoat instead of plain epoxy for that reason. DIY roll-on kits on unground concrete often start peeling in the first year.

Will hot tires peel an epoxy garage floor?

Hot tire pickup happens when a tire softens a thin or poorly bonded coating and lifts it off the slab. After a summer drive on SR-95 your tires can be hot enough to do it to a hardware-store kit. Diamond grinding the concrete first and using a full-thickness base coat is what stops it.

Can you install epoxy flooring in the summer heat?

Yes, but the schedule changes. Epoxy and polyaspartic cure faster as the slab gets hotter, so in June through September we start at first light and work while the concrete is still cool. October through April is the easiest season to coat a garage floor here, and it books up first.

Epoxy or polyaspartic: which is better for an Arizona garage?

Plain epoxy is strong and bonds well, but it yellows (ambers) in sunlight. Polyaspartic is UV-stable, cures in hours and handles temperature swings better. Most garage systems we install use an epoxy or polyaspartic base, a flake broadcast and a polyaspartic clear topcoat, so you get both.

How long before I can park in my garage after a floor coating?

You can usually walk on a one-day polyaspartic floor that evening and park on it in about 24 to 48 hours. A full epoxy system takes longer, often three to five days before heavy vehicles. Boats on trailers and RVs should wait the full cure time for the system we used.

Do you coat floors with cracks or old paint on them?

Yes. Cracks get ground out and filled before coating, and old paint or a failed DIY coating has to be ground off completely, because a new coating is only as good as what it sticks to. A slab with an active moisture problem is the one case where we may tell you to hold off and fix the water first.
